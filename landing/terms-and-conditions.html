2. Service Description

Mobio is a peer-to-peer mobility marketplace that connects passengers with independent drivers in Rwanda. The App provides:

  • Driver Discovery: Real-time listing of nearby motos, cabs, trikes, and trucks.
  • Trip Scheduling: The ability to create and manage scheduled trips with specific routes, dates, and times.
  • WhatsApp Connect: Direct communication between passengers and drivers via WhatsApp.
  • BioPay: Biometric-enabled payment facilitation using face recognition and QR codes for MTN MoMo transactions.

Mobio acts as a marketplace platform only. We do not provide transportation services directly and are not a transportation carrier. All transportation is provided by independent drivers.
