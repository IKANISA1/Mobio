2. Service Description

Mobio is a peer-to-peer mobility discovery marketplace that helps drivers and passengers in Rwanda coordinate directly. The App provides:

  • Nearby Discovery: Listing of nearby drivers or passengers who choose to be visible.

Mobio acts as a marketplace platform only. We do not provide transportation services directly and are not a transportation carrier. All transportation is provided by independent drivers.
