Good to know

A few details
before you go.

How do I arrange a trip?

Browse nearby contacts or scheduled trip posts, open a profile and use chat or WhatsApp to agree the details. Drivers and passengers arrange the journey directly.

Who sets the fare?

You and your contact agree the fare directly. Mobio does not provide automatic fare quotes or dispatch a driver.

When is my location visible?

Location access is controlled by your phone’s permissions. Driver visibility is controlled by the online switch in Mobio; go offline to stop sharing.

How can I manage my account data?

Read our privacy policy or follow the account deletion instructions.
