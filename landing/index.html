Features

to move around

Discover nearby mobility contacts, publish scheduled posts, and chat securely.

Nearby Discovery

See nearby drivers or passengers who choose to be visible. Browse profile and vehicle details before contacting them.

Scheduled Posts

Create and view planned trip posts with route labels, time, vehicle type, and notes.

Direct Communication

Message through Mobio or hand off to WhatsApp when a profile allows it.

Marketplace Scope

Discovery and communication.
No in-app payment flow.

Mobio is not a taxi dispatch, fare quote, live route tracking, or payment settlement service. Users agree on fares and trip details manually outside the app.
