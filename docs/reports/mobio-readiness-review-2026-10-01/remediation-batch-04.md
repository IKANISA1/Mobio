Durable role batches copy their reviewed tuples and retain independent receipts; expiring the cache cannot cascade into those records.

Role selection prepares a local frozen review. Cancel has no target-role effect. The server checks current authority before and after its actor fence, uses a monotonically advancing role revision to reject changed targets, and commits a role change, its audit entry and recipient receipt atomically. Own/same-role profiles are excluded. Changing a profile role does not create trusted Admin access. Confirmed, known-rejected, unknown, not-started and skipped outcomes remain distinct. Status reads and explicit closure reconcile uncertainty without replaying a role request; sealing an absent reservation prevents a delayed submission from creating work afterward.
