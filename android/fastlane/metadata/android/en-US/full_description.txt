Mobio is a Pan-African peer-to-peer ride-sharing marketplace, launching first in Rwanda.

Get rides instantly — no sign-up, no login wall. Just open the app and go.

KEY FEATURES

🚗 Real-Time Driver Discovery
Find available drivers near you with live status updates and real-time location tracking.

📅 Trip Scheduling
Book rides in advance — set your pickup location, date, and time for guaranteed availability.

🔐 BioPay — Biometric Payments
Secure, contactless payments powered by facial recognition. No cards, no PINs, no hassle.

📋 Trip Management
View trip details, track your booking history, and manage all your rides from one place.

🎨 Premium Design
Beautiful minimalist Claymorphic interface with smooth animations and dual light/dark themes for a native app-like experience.

⚡ Instant Access
Anonymous authentication gets you riding in seconds. No personal data required to start.

🇷🇼 Made for Rwanda
Default country, currency (RWF), and MTN MoMo payment support built in from day one. Pan-African expansion coming soon.

PAYMENT OPTIONS
• Cash — pay your driver directly
• MTN MoMo — USSD-based mobile money (Rwanda)
• BioPay — biometric face authentication
